---

## Reporting an Issue

When reporting an issue, please include:

1. **Rosh version:** `rosh --version`
2. **Minimal code** to reproduce the issue
3. **Expected behavior** vs **actual behavior**
4. **Target platform** (if applicable): Phaser, Pygame, Three.js
